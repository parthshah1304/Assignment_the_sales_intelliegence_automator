Relevant B2B lead = a company whose main revenue comes from selling products or
services to OTHER BUSINESSES or ORGANIZATIONS (commercial property owners, property
managers, HOAs, contractors, municipalities, schools, wholesalers, other companies).

Answer "No" when the company serves mainly individual consumers / homeowners
(e.g. residential-only services, retail shops, restaurants, bakeries).

Mixed businesses: answer "Yes" ONLY if the website explicitly offers a commercial /
business / industrial / wholesale line of service. "We serve everyone" is not enough.
If the evidence is thin or ambiguous, answer "No" and lower the confidence.
